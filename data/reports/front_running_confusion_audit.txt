- Patterns higher in Front Running FP than TP suggest false-positive drivers: call_before_state_write, value_or_gas_sensitive_call.

- Front Running FP top chunks contain generic risk evidence (has_external_call), which is not specific enough to distinguish transaction-order dependence from Access/Reentrancy/Time behavior.
