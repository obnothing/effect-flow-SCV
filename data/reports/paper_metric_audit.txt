Why our results may differ from the paper
- The paper does not state the exact BERT backbone name or tokenizer implementation.
- The paper does not state max sequence length or truncation/chunking policy.
- The paper does not state threshold selection for multi-label outputs.
- The paper does not state train/test split ratio, validation use, random seed, or opcode-hash duplicate control.
- Random split has opcode-hash overlap and raises our score, but remains non-strict.
- Average F1 likely means averaging ten per-vulnerability rows, not strict multi-label micro-F1.
- BJUT label mapping is uncertain, especially the Unchecked call return value mapping.
- Small support labels heavily affect macro-style averages.
- Opcode-only input can miss source-level semantics and control/data-flow evidence.
