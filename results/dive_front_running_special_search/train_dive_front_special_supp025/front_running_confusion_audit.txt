- Front Running FP contracts frequently carry other positive labels: Access Control true_rate=0.937500, Reentrancy true_rate=0.562500, Time manipulation true_rate=0.250000.
- Front Running FN contracts also overlap with high-prevalence labels: Access Control true_rate=1.000000, Reentrancy true_rate=0.727273, Time manipulation true_rate=0.590909.
- Patterns lower in Front Running FN than TP suggest missing true-positive signals: sensitive_call_with_auth_guard, call_with_return_check, has_external_call, state_write_before_call, div_before_mul.
- Front Running FP top chunks contain generic risk evidence (has_external_call), which is not specific enough to distinguish transaction-order dependence from Access/Reentrancy/Time behavior.
- Recommended next step: keep Front Running risk evidence conservative and add more specific order/value/state-update proxies before increasing its side-evidence weight.
